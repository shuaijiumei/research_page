About Me

I am a first-year PhD student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), advised by Prof. Youcheng Sun. I received my Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), where I was fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Pinjia He and collaborate closely with Junjielong Xu. My research interests lie at the intersection of Large Language Models and Software Engineering (LLM4SE), with a particular focus on leveraging LLMs to address critical software engineering challenges, including intelligent code generation, automated program repair, and comprehensive log analysis.

I am actively seeking research collaborators. If you are interested in my research or would like to explore potential collaborations, please feel free to reach out via email: boyintan1221@gmail.com.
